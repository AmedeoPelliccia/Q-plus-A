# 01-01-01 Technical Divisions

This group contains the technical, scientific and engineering capability divisions responsible for product, technology, research and infrastructure development within Q+A.

## Scope

Technical divisions own the engineering, scientific and computational disciplines that produce governed artefacts — CAD models, simulations, analyses, data modules, technical publications and evidence packages — against the Q+A product and technology breakdown structures.
