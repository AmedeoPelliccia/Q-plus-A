# 01-01-02 Enterprise Divisions

This group contains enterprise governance, support, organizational, supplier-management and authority-interface functions within Q+A.

## Scope

Enterprise divisions provide the governance, compliance, financial, legal, human-resources, risk, ESG, DEI, programme management, procurement and regulatory-interface functions that enable the technical divisions to operate within a controlled and accountable organizational framework.

## Scope of 01-01-02-11 Authorities and Regulators Interfaces

This division manages interfaces and coordination with:

* aviation and space authorities (EASA, FAA, ECSS, etc.);
* certification bodies;
* standards organizations;
* public institutions and regulators;
* external scientific and research institutions;
* independent experts and advisory bodies;
* institutional research programmes.

> **Note.** This division manages interfaces and coordination. It does not replace the technical ownership of research performed by [`Q-SCIRES`](../01-01-01_TECHNICAL-DIVISIONS/01-01-01-11_Q-SCIRES/). The former `01-01-01-12_Q-HUESCORT-SCIRES-OPEN` division has been superseded; its authority- and regulator-interface responsibilities are now managed by this division.
