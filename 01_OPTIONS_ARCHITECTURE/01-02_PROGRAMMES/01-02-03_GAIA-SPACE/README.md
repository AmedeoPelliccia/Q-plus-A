# GAIA-SPACE — Programme

Space research, exploration and technology under the **[Charter for Shared Horizons](GAIA-SPACE-CHARTER_Shared-Horizons.md)** (Issue 0.2, PROPOSED): explore the Universe to widen shared knowledge, and take responsibility for the consequences on Earth and in space. Missions are selected and reviewed on five dimensions — scientific, social, technological, ecological, long-term — none of which compensates a serious violation in another.

| Folder | Role |

| [`01-02-03-01_PRODUCTS`](01-02-03-01_PRODUCTS/) | one SBS per mission model |
| [`01-02-03-02_CERTIFICATION`](01-02-03-02_CERTIFICATION/) | applicable regimes and compliance evidence |
| [`01-02-03-03_LIFECYCLE-GATES`](01-02-03-03_LIFECYCLE-GATES/) | review outcomes — ALLOW · WARN · ESCALATE · BLOCK — bound to integrated baselines |
| [`01-02-03-04_IMPACT-STUDIES`](01-02-03-04_IMPACT-STUDIES/) | five-dimension assessments and the scientific, economic-social and financial ledgers |
