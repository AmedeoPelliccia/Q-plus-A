# CONOPS-EWTW-ECS-001 — Concept of operations of the environmental control system

**Version:** 0.1.1 · **Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Status:** first issue, proposed
**For:** `KNOT-EWTW-SRC-001`, `KNU-EWTW-SRC-001-01` Electrical load analysis
**Input:** [`conops.yaml`](conops.yaml) · **Checked by:** [`tools/conops_check.py`](../../../../../../../../../tools/conops_check.py)

This document says how the environmental control system of the eWTW is used: in which phases it works, what it does, what supplies it, and what it does when something fails. The authority asked for it on 2026-11-14.

It carries no figure of power, and the tool refuses one. The research found no load profile by phase for an aircraft of this class ([record of the sources](../KNOT-EWTW-SRC-001_sources-and-storage_2026-11-14.md), findings 18 and 19). The profile is ours to build, and this document is its first part: it fixes the phases and the cases for which the load analysis has to give a number.

It states no conformity with any rule.

---

## What it rests on

Three things were already on record:

- **The system is declared.** The model declares an electric system without bleed air, with electric packs in an unpressurised bay of the wing-to-fuselage fairing ([model README](../../../../README.md), section 3, row 9).
- **The source is fixed.** A turbogenerator is a main source in every option since ruling R1 of `KNOT-EWTW-SRC-001`.
- **The essential case is assumed.** The [essential load analysis](../ELA-EWTW-ESS-001/README.md) assumes that the alternate source does not pressurise the cabin and that the aircraft descends.

The rest is proposed here or open. Each statement below says which.

## What is not known yet

Three open inputs decide most of the table, and none is decided in this document:

1. **The cruise altitude and the cabin altitude** (`KNOT-EWTW-CRZ-001`). They set the pressure ratio of the compression.
2. **What supplies the packs at the stand.** No KNOT holds the auxiliary power module.
3. **What is shed when a turbogenerator is lost** (`KNOT-EWTW-GEN-001`). It follows the number of turbogenerators.

One point is for the author of the taxonomy: S-ATLAS `021` names no node for compressing outside air to cabin pressure, which is the source of pressurisation in an aircraft without bleed air.
