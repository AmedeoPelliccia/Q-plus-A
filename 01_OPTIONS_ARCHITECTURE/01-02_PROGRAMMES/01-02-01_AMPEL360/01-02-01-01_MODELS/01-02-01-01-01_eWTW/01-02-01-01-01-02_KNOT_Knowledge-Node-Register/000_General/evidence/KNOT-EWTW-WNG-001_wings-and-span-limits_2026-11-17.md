## What follows for the eWTW

Figures in this section that are not in a statement above are arithmetic of the reviewer.

- **The class is well inside letter C.** The three types read have spans of 27.05 to 28.72 m, against a limit of 36 m (statements 1 to 4). The limit is now read at a source; the context of the KNOT said it was not.
- **Letter C leaves room for a much higher aspect ratio.** A span just under 36 m gives an aspect ratio of 14.0 on a wing of 92.5 m², the area of the Embraer 190, and of 21.2 on a wing of 61 m², the area of the ATR 72-600 ([record of the fuel location](KNOT-EWTW-FUL-001_fuel-location_2026-11-15.md), statement 1). Option B of this KNOT, a higher aspect ratio on a smaller area, does not need option C before those values. Folding tips or a brace are needed only beyond them.
- **The turboprop wing and the jet wing differ by a factor in loading.** At maximum take-off mass the ATR 72-600 carries 377 kg/m² on an aspect ratio of 12.0, and takes off in 1,315 m. The Embraer 190 carries 517 to 560 kg/m² on a reference aspect ratio of 8.1. The cruise point decides between the two, which is why this KNOT is coupled with `KNOT-EWTW-CRZ-001`.
- **The fuel a wing holds follows its area, and not in proportion.** The Embraer 190 holds 12,971 kg in 92.5 m², 140 kg/m². The ATR 72-600 holds 5,000 kg in 61 m², 82 kg/m². The requirement mission needs 6.8 to 7.7 t. A wing sized as a turboprop wing may not hold it; that is the first check of `KNU-EWTW-WNG-001-02`, and it decides option A of `KNOT-EWTW-FUL-001`.
- **A brace buys 5 to 11 % over a wing that is already of high aspect ratio, on paper.** The comparison of statement 5 is against a cantilever wing of aspect ratio 13 with folding tips, not against a wing of the class. It is calculated, for 154 passengers, and supported by wind-tunnel tests only (statement 6). Nothing is read for 100 seats.
- **No flight result supports a gain from propellers on the wing at this scale.** The aircraft the report cites for it never flew (statement 7). The one flight campaign on record is the six propellers of 50 kW in the evidence of maturity record `Q2` of `KNOT-EWTW-CRZ-001`. The coupling with `KNOT-EWTW-PRP-001` stays an assumption: the effect is to be shown.

These are consequences drawn by the reviewer. They are not quotations.

## Open

Research can still give:

- the wing area of the Dash 8-400, and the span and wing area of the Airbus A220 and of the Embraer E2 family, read at the manufacturer;
- the take-off and landing field lengths of the Embraer 190, with their conditions;
- the stands that operators of 70 to 100 seats use, by survey of airports;
- a study of a wing of high aspect ratio or of a braced wing for 50 to 100 seats, by title, with the mass of the wing;
- a flight or wind-tunnel result, by title, for propellers at the wingtips at a power of megawatts.

The rest is work of the programme and decisions of the authority:

- the wing area and the span each cruise point gives, and the fuel the box holds (`KNU-EWTW-WNG-001-02`);
- the limit on the span as a criterion, and the other criteria of this KNOT (`KNU-EWTW-WNG-001-03`).
