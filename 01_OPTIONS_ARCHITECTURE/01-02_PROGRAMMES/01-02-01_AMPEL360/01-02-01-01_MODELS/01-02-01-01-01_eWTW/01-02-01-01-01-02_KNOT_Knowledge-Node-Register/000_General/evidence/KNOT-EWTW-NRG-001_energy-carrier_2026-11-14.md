## What follows for the eWTW

- **The final reserve of an all-electric eWTW has no value in the rule.** Statement 2 gives a time for two kinds of engine, and an electric engine is neither. For option A the time is a point for the authority, to be settled before the mission is approved. For options B and C the reserve of a turbine-engined aeroplane applies if the thermal machine is a turbine, which is to be confirmed.
- **The design range is a decision, and the market gives two very different numbers.** The range offered by a type of the class is about six times the average stage its fleet flies (statements 4 and 5). Neither is a design mission for the eWTW.
- **Nothing read shows a benefit at this size.** In the nearest class studied, a turbofan of 78 passengers, hybrid propulsion showed no fuel or energy benefit at 500 Wh/kg (statement 7). The studies that close are of smaller aircraft, on shorter missions, at specific energies that are assumptions.
- **A cell figure is not a pack figure.** The projections of statement 10 are at cell level, and the relation to the pack is not constant (statement 9). The one pack figure read is a laboratory pack over 25 cycles (statement 11). A figure enters the trade labelled as cell or pack, demonstrated or projected, with its date.
- **The megawatt class exists on the bench as motors.** No turbogenerator was read at the power of this aircraft, and none in airline service as a hybrid powerplant.
- **The order of work is mission, budget, trade.** With no published budget for the class (statement 20), the budget is ours to build on the approved mission, with every assumption stated. Scoring options A, B and C before the mission is approved would build its assumption into the ruling.

These are consequences drawn by the reviewer. They are not quotations.

## Open

Research can still give:

- the stage lengths of the European fleet of the class, from a source with its unit;
- what the acceptable means of compliance say on the final reserve, and any position of EASA for an aeroplane with electric engines;
- the specific energy of packs that are certificated or have flown, with the document that states it;
- the mass and the fuel consumption of turbine engines of the class a turbogenerator would use, from type-certificate data sheets.

The rest is work of the programme and decisions of the authority:

- the design mission and the reserve policy (`KNU-EWTW-NRG-001-02`);
- the budget of energy and power on that mission (`KNU-EWTW-NRG-001-03`);
- approval of the criteria, with weights, thresholds and the maturity required of each critical element (`KNU-EWTW-NRG-001-01`);
- the rationale and the evidence of ruling R1 (`KNU-EWTW-NRG-001-07`).
