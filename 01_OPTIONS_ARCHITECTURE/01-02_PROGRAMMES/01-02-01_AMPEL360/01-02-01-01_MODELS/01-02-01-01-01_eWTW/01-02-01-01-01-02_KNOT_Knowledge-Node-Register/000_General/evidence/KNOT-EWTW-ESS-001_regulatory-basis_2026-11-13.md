*As reported* means that the reviewer did not read the clause again on that date. A statement is read at clause level, against the amendment of the certification basis, before a criterion that rests on it is approved.

## What follows for the eWTW

On an aircraft with electric propulsion, the generators or the batteries of the propulsion chain are also the normal electrical sources. The loss of normal generation can then coincide with the loss of thrust. An essential source that shares the electrical path of propulsion is not independent of the failure it has to survive. This is a consequence drawn from statements 1, 3 and 10. It is not a quotation of a rule.

## Open

- The essential loads of the eWTW in kilowatts, and a method to estimate them.
- The emergency sources of the regional class, from sources that can be checked.
- Whether an authority has accepted a propulsion battery, or a generator of the propulsion chain, as the alternate source.
- Weights and thresholds of the tradable criteria, and the maturity required of each source.

These points are the research briefs of `KNU-EWTW-ESS-001-01`, `-02` and `-03` as revised on 2026-11-13.
