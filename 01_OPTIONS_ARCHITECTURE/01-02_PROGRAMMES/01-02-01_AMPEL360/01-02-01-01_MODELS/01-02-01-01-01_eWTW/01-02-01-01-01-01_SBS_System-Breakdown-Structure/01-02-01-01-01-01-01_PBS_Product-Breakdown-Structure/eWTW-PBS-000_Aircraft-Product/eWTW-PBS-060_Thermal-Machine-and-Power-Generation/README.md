# eWTW-PBS-060 — Thermal Machine and Power Generation

Product group. Realizes S-ATLAS band `060-069` (mapping per AMPEL360-PBS-PN-CM-001 §3.1; PBS codes are PBS-local). Chapters:

- [`eWTW-PBS-068-000`](eWTW-PBS-068-000_Turbogenerator-Installation) — Turbogenerator Installation

Opened on 2026-11-14 at the request of the authority (`KNU-EWTW-PRP-001-05`), ahead of the ruling of `KNOT-EWTW-PRP-001`, which has none yet. Ruling R2 of `KNOT-EWTW-NRG-001` leaves two options, and both carry conventional fuel for a thermal machine that generates electricity. Ruling R3 of that KNOT and ruling R1 of `KNOT-EWTW-SRC-001`, of the same day, authorise the turbogenerator installation, which every option holds. The group is retired if the thermal machine leaves the option set by a reopen condition. The propulsors are electric in both options and stay in group `eWTW-PBS-070`.

Only the chapter that both options need is opened. The other chapters of the band are opened when the machine is defined: its installation, the machine itself, carrier delivery, air and thermal management, control, exhaust, lubrication and starting. The number, the rating and the position of the turbogenerators are open (`KNOT-EWTW-GEN-001`, decided with `KNOT-EWTW-PRP-001`).
