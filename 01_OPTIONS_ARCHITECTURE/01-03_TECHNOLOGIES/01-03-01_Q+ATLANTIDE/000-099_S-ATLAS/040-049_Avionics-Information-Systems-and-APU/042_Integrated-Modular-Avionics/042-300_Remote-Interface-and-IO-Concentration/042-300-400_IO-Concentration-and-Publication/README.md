# 042-300-400 — IO Concentration and Publication

**Node:** 042-300_Remote-Interface-and-IO-Concentration · **Subject:** 004

- Concentration groups channels into network publications by consumer need and refresh class, not by physical adjacency alone; grouping is a configuration-controlled decision.
- Publication properties (refresh rate, grouping, freshness marking) are declared per publication and consumed as contracts under 042-200 governance.
- Concentration ratios and their latency cost are declared unit properties; headroom for added channels and publications is a growth reserve.
