# 042-900-009 — Evidence and Certification Data

**Node:** 042-900_Platform-Health-and-Resource-Management · **Subject:** 009

- Evidence set: detection-coverage substantiation per failure class, false-alarm and missed-detection analyses, containment verification per region, reconfiguration verification per pre-qualified state and transition, and the configuration index per loaded standard.
- Records produced in service (transitions, margin trends) are themselves evidence-grade: complete, time-based, version-stamped.
- Programme applicability, effectivity and instance sizing are out of scope for the taxonomy: they are declared in the programme impact studies and PBS layers that map onto this subject.
