## Environmental and Weather Limitations

Neutral categories of environmental operating constraint:

- Temperature envelope (ambient and operating)
- Wind, crosswind, and gust limits
- Icing, precipitation, and contamination limits
- Energy-carrier-specific environmental constraints *(bound per programme via `002-900`)*

> Programme binding may tighten or extend these limits for a specific energy carrier
> (e.g. cryogenic LH₂ thermal constraints, high-voltage battery temperature thresholds).
