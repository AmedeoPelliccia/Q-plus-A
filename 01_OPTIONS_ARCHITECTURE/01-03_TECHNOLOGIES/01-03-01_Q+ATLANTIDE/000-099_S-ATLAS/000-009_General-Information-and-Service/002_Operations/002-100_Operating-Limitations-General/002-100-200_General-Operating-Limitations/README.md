## General Operating Limitations

Architecture-neutral categories of operating limitation that every programme instantiates:

- Speed and load-factor limits
- Weight and centre-of-gravity limits *(energy-carrier mass/CG effects bound at `008-900`)*
- Altitude and pressurisation limits
- Energy-carrier operating limits *(bound at `002-900` / `004-900` per programme)*

> Values are programme-specific and bound downstream; this node defines the neutral slots only.
