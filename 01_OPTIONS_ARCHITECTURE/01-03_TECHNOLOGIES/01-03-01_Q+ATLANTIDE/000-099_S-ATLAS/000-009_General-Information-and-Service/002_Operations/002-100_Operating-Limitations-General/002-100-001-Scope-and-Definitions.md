# 002-100-001 — Scope and Definitions

**Item:** `002-100-001` ⇄ ATA **02-10-01**
**Node:** `002-100_Operating-Limitations-General`
**Owner:** Q-AIR · **Status:** agnostic standard (SSOT)

## Scope

Defines the boundary of the operating-limitations node: which classes of limit are
standardised here in architecture-neutral terms, and which are deferred to programme
binding via the `00X-900` green-architecture delta or to other nodes.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Operating limitation | A constraint on the operation of the product that must not be exceeded in service. |
| Agnostic slot | A neutral function placeholder bound to a real technology by a programme. |
| Programme binding | Mapping of an agnostic slot to a specific energy carrier / geometry via impact study. |
