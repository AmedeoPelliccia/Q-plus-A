# 054-600 — Fire Thermal and Acoustic Protection Structures

**Chapter:** 054_Nacelles-Pylons-and-Propulsion-Integration-Structures · **Section:** 600

- Protection structure of the installation: firewall panels and seals as structure, thermal barriers, acoustic treatment structure.
- This section owns the physical implementation of fire barriers, thermal shields, acoustic liners, insulation supports and structural segregation provisions. It does not own hazard detection, extinguishing, environmental qualification or source-noise functions (026, 061-300, 067-600 and the environmental chapters own those).
