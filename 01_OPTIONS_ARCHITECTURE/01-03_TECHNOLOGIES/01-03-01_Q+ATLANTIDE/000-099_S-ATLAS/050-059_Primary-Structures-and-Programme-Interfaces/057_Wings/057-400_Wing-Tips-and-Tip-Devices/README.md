# 057-400 — Wing Tips and Tip Devices

**Chapter:** 057_Wings · **Section:** 400

- Tip structure and tip devices: winglets and other tip aerodynamic devices, their internal structure, fairings and provisions for adaptive or folding tips.
- Folding-tip ground-compatibility constraints are class matter (09x-700); the mechanism-bearing structure is owned here.
