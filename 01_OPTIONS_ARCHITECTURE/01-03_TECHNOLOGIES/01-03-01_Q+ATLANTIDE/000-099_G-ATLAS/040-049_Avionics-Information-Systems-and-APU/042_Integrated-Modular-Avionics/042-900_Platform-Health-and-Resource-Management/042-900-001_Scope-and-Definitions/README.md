# 042-900-001 — Scope and Definitions

**Node:** 042-900_Platform-Health-and-Resource-Management · **Subject:** 001

- Health event: a detected deviation with declared source, severity class and containment consequence.
- Fault containment region: the smallest platform element whose failure is prevented from propagating — aligned to partitions, modules, network planes and remote units.
- Pre-qualified configuration: an integrated configuration state verified in advance (042-400 evidence) and eligible as a reconfiguration target.
- Margin surveillance: runtime comparison of observed resource use against allocated budgets and declared margins.
- Out of scope: BITE mechanisms themselves (owned by their source nodes), hosting and budget policy (042-400), maintenance analytics and prognostics (045), crew alerting logic (flight-deck indicating chapters).
