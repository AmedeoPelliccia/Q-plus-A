# 100-300 — Range Register and Navigation

**Chapter:** 100_General-and-Band-Doctrine · **Section:** 300

The ten ranges of the band and their reading order: 100-109 the transversal engineering-assurance-and-human-support layer; 110-189 the functional domains (structures-materials-mechanisms, propulsion, power-and-thermal, avionics-GNC-autonomy, communications-and-data, sensors-payloads-and-downstream, on-orbit operations-servicing-and-maintenance, ground-segment-launch-and-recovery); 190-199 the system and vehicle classes with the expansion register. Chapter registers are ratified (ruling §4); technology sections derive from the standards register per range.

*Subjects are register-gated and follow per-range ratification.*
