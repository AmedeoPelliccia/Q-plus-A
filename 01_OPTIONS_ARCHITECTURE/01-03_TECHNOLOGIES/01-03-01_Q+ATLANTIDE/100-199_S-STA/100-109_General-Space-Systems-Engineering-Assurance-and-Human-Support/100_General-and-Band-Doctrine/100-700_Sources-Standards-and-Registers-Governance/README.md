# 100-700 — Sources Standards and Registers Governance

**Chapter:** 100_General-and-Band-Doctrine · **Section:** 700

Sources inspire structure and never govern it; standards are referenced undated by number (the undated reference designates the current edition; currency is verified at citation); manuals and books are never named in taxonomy content and never reproduced; crosswalks map, they never govern. The machine-readable standards register (metadata only, no normative text) gates technology sections; ISO/TC20/SC14 is the seeded backbone, ISO/TC20/SC13 with CCSDS and the ECSS E/Q/M branches join on acquisition. The band SOURCES file records provenance per source with its usage class. Documentation-precedence doctrine is band governance anchored here and in 103 (ISO 10789): a declared hierarchy from synthesis documents through operational rules and procedures to source data, so every operational statement has one authoritative home and derived views declare their sources.

*Subjects are register-gated and follow per-range ratification.*
