# 100-000 — General Information

**Chapter:** 100_General-and-Band-Doctrine · **Section:** 000

How to read the band: chapter roles, the transversal range as the band's General layer (no per-range General chapter — a deliberate deviation from the aircraft band's 060-099 pattern, because this range serves all ten), and the relationship between doctrine chapters and register-gated technology chapters.

*Subjects are register-gated and follow per-range ratification.*
