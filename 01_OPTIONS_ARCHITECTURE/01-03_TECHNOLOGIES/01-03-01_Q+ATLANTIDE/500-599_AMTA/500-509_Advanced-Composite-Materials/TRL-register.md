## 2. Controlled Rules

- TRL values in this register are **evidence-based maturity claims**.
- TRL values that are not supported by evidence are **provisional**.
- Architecture-level TRL is **programme-agnostic**.
- Programme-specific TRL must be recorded separately **with effectivity**
  (programme, product, configuration, lifecycle state, evidence).
- TRL measures technology maturity only; it does not measure product, CAD, or
  configuration maturity (see the root control folder).
