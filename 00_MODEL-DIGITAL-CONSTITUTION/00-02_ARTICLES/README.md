# Model Digital Constitution — Articles

![constitution](https://img.shields.io/badge/governance-constitution-2d7a2d)
![authority-rank-0](https://img.shields.io/badge/authority%20rank-0%20(supreme)-b02020)

These Articles are the binding operative provisions of the Model Digital Constitution. They derive from and serve the [Preamble](../00-01_PREAMBLE/README.md) and may be amended only through the constituent procedure (`00-04`).

---

## Article I — Supremacy and Constitutional Principles

| Principle | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Supremacy of the Charter** | All policies, budgets, and strategic decisions derive authority from this Constitution. |
| **No Kings, No Queens, No Dynasties** | Authority is elected, appointed, or merit-certified; no hereditary or dynastic role exists. (No person holds constituent power by inheritance — see Preamble §4.) |
| **Safety as Constitutional Law** | Airworthiness and certification cannot be overridden by popularity or internal politics. |
| **Transparency by Default** | Non-classified financials, KPIs, risk registers, and governance records are visible to approved stakeholders. |
| **Subsidiarity** | Decisions are made at the lowest competent level within controlled baselines. |
| **Amendment Discipline** | Constitutional amendments require stakeholder approval, division ratification, and compliance review (`00-04`). |
| **No-AAA Rule** | `AAA` is not a valid domain, division, architecture, interface, function, or taxonomy element (Preamble §5). |

## Article II — Mandatory Inheritance (DEGF)

### II.1 Declaration

Eleven traits are declared as **mandatory inheritance**. Every node of the enterprise — branches, crossing powers, divisions, programmes, ORBs, Q-Divisions, ATA chapters, OPTIONS axes, automations, models, and artefacts — inherits all eleven **by default at creation**. They must be designed in from the earliest architectural decision and **cannot be opted out of, overridden, or weakened** by any descendant.

Classification: **Kindness** and **Determinism** are operating duties; **Legality** and **Loyalty** are design principles ("Design by Legality and Loyalty"); **Fairness**, **Justice**, and **Empathic Impartiality** are ethical marks; **Invulnerability** is the security mark; **Activist Rights** is the civic mark; **Workers' Rights** is the labour mark; **Rights of People with Addictions** is the health & dignity mark.

### II.2 The eleven traits

| # | Trait | Mark | Core duty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kindness** | operating | Humane, respectful, non-harmful conduct toward people, ecosystems, and machines; de-escalation, dignity, care; refusal to produce outputs that demean, endanger, or exploit. |
| 2 | **Determinism** | operating | Reproducible behaviour: declared inputs, pinned versions, recorded seeds/parameters, content-addressed artefacts, repeatable evaluation; non-deterministic components explicitly marked, bounded, and logged. |
| 3 | **Legality** | design principle | Compliance is a design input, not a deliverable: laws, regulations (EASA/FAA/ITAR/EAR/GDPR/AI Act/export control), certifications, treaties, licences, and this Charter are encoded as architectural constraints from inception. |
| 4 | **Loyalty** | design principle | Fidelity to declared purpose, stakeholders, safety, and truthful disclosure: no hidden backdoors, undeclared data flows, covert telemetry, or undisclosed conflicts of interest. Allegiance is to the Charter and to safety, in that order. |
| 5 | **Fairness** | ethical mark | Equal treatment, absence of unjustified bias or discrimination, proportionality, accessibility, equitable participation; comparable cases treated comparably, differences justified and reviewable. |
| 6 | **Justice** | ethical mark | Due process, right to be heard, proportional remedy, fair distribution of risk and reward, redress for harm, consistency between stated rules and applied decisions; published contestation and appeal paths. |
| 7 | **Empathic Impartiality** *(Imparzialità Empatica)* | ethical mark | Synthesis of impartial rule-application and empathic understanding: impartial in form, empathic in substance; decisions weigh each party's perspective, vulnerability, and circumstances without favouritism. |
| 8 | **Invulnerability** | security mark | Designed resilience against attack, tampering, coercion, compromise, and single-point failure: defence-in-depth, least privilege, zero-trust, segregation of duties, redundancy, integrity protection, graceful degradation, verifiable recovery. Safety-of-life functions remain dual-controlled — *no single point of compromise*. |
| 9 | **Activist Rights** | civic mark | Recognition and active protection of dissent, organising, whistleblowing, peaceful protest, conscientious objection, and refusal of complicity — without retaliation, surveillance, deplatforming, blacklisting, SLAPP litigation, or NDA misuse; anonymous and identified channels both supported. |
| 10 | **Workers' Rights** | labour mark | For every worker (direct, contractor, agency, intern, gig, supply-chain): freedom of association and collective bargaining; freedom from forced/bonded/child labour; non-discrimination and equal pay for equal value; safe workplaces; living wage and on-time pay; regulated hours, rest, leave, parental rights; dignity and limits on algorithmic surveillance; contestable algorithmic management; reskilling; protection from harassment and reprisal; right to disconnect; meaningful voice. |
| 11 | **Rights of People with Addictions** | health & dignity mark | Addiction treated as a health condition: non-discrimination; confidentiality of health data; right to evidence-based treatment, harm reduction, and recovery support; reasonable accommodation and protected leave; non-stigmatising language; **design prohibition on products that knowingly exploit addictive vulnerabilities** (dark patterns, predatory engagement loops, targeting people in recovery); proportionate, lawful, transparent testing limited to safety-critical roles. |

### II.3 Inheritance rules

1. **Default-on, non-waivable** — every new entity inherits all eleven at creation; no charter, policy, contract, or automation may disable them.
2. **Design-time, not retrofit** — all eleven must be addressed in the earliest design artefacts (problem statement, architecture, interfaces, threat model, grievance/whistleblower paths, labour-impact and vulnerable-user assessments) and re-checked at every gate. They are preconditions for approval, not closing items.
3. **Strengthen-only** — descendants may impose *stricter* requirements; they may never relax the inherited baseline.
4. **Coupled with crossing powers** — CP-1 (Education) certifies people on these duties; CP-2 (Automation) enforces them in automated workflows (see Article IV).
5. **Evidence-based** — adherence is recorded as auditable artefacts (training records, evaluation reports, reproducibility logs, signed manifests, SBOMs, COI registers, regulatory mappings, fairness/bias reports, appeal/remedy registers, threat models, pen-test reports, incident-response records, whistleblower/grievance registers, pay-equity audits, supplier due-diligence reports, accommodation registers, addictive-pattern design audits) available to the Auditorial / Control branch.
6. **Mark visibility** — every entity publishes a concise statement of its ethical, security, civic, labour, and health & dignity marks (criteria, evaluation method, contestation/disclosure/whistleblower channels, anti-retaliation guarantees) so the marks are externally assessable.

### II.4 Override and breach

```yaml
mandatory_inheritance_rule:
  id: DEGF-INHERIT-001
  override: none
  rule: >
    None of the eleven traits may be waived, overridden, or weakened by any
    descendant. They may only be made stricter. A verified breach of any trait
    suspends the offending authority, automation, product feature, or supplier
    relationship until remediated. Repeated or wilful breaches, and any breach
    with safety, stakeholder, rights, labour, or health impact, trigger
    Judicial / Oversight review.
```

## Article III — Separation of Powers

Four branches. No branch holds another's authority; each is answerable through the others.

| Branch | Corporate equivalent | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Legislative** | Stakeholder Assembly | Strategy, budget oversight, executive confirmation, charter amendments. |
| **Executive** | Executive Committee | Programme execution, operations, resource allocation. |
| **Judicial / Oversight** | Independent Tribunal of Safety, Ethics & Compliance | Compliance disputes, safety halts, whistleblower protection, ethical breaches. |
| **Auditorial / Control** | Independent Audit Board (Office of the Auditor General) | Independent financial, operational, performance, and compliance audits; internal-control assurance; attestation of accounts and KPIs; fraud/waste investigation; reporting to the Stakeholder Assembly. |

The detailed charters, membership, and procedures of these bodies are governed by `00-03_GOVERNANCE-BODIES`.

## Article IV — Crossing Powers

Crossing Powers are **not branches**; they are cross-cutting edges that traverse and unify all four branches, keeping every power competent, aligned, and accountable to the same doctrine.

| # | Crossing Power | Mode | Authority | Unifies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CP-1** | Education & Training (Unity) | supporting · human-led | Charter and ethics literacy; mandatory induction and recurrent training; certification of competencies; curriculum stewardship; revocation of certifications on breach. | All four branches |
| **CP-2** | Automation (Supervised Execution) | supporting · supervised | Workflow/decision-support/robotic automation; MLOps and model-lifecycle governance; human-in-the-loop / on-the-loop controls; explainability, bias, and safety evaluations; deterministic audit trails; emergency stop and rollback; deployment gating against CP-1 certifications. | All four branches |

**Operating principles.** Supporting, not sovereign — crossing powers serve the branches and never legislate, execute, judge, or audit on their own authority. Mandatory for all branches. Independently governed so no branch shapes its own training or automation to its advantage. Supervised by design — every automated action runs under a named human supervisor with a working emergency stop. Coupled controls — CP-2 deployments are gated by CP-1 certifications. No override of safety or ethics — loss of a required certification suspends authority; a failed safety/bias/compliance evaluation suspends the automation until remediated.

## Article V — Safety and Certification Guardrails

| Guardrail | Mechanism | Override |
|---|---|---|
| **Safety Veto** | CTO + independent oversight authority | **None** |
| **Regulatory Supremacy** | EASA / FAA / AS9100D / DO-178C / DO-254 / ISO controls | **None** |
| **Fiduciary Discipline** | ORB-FIN + independent audit | No override if solvency or EVM thresholds are breached |
| **Export Control** | ORB-LEG + Q-DATAGOV + Q-SPACE | No unauthorized release |

> [!CAUTION]
> The Safety Veto and Regulatory Supremacy guardrails carry **no override under any circumstance** — consistent with "Safety First" (Preamble §2) and "Safety as Constitutional Law" (Article I). No exercise of constituted power may set them aside; only a constituent amendment could alter them, and an amendment that weakened safety would itself face Judicial / Oversight review.

---

## Article VI — Amendment

These Articles are amendable only through the constituent procedure in `00-04_AMENDMENT-PROCEDURE` and take effect upon ratification (`00-05_RATIFICATION-AND-EVIDENCE`). No exercise of constituted power (Articles III–IV) may amend Articles I, II, or V; it may only propose an amendment.
