## Preamble

We who conceive, design, build, verify, certify, operate, maintain, and retire the products of **Q-plus** — recognizing that aerospace technology carries human lives and shapes the shared environment, and that this responsibility outlives any single decision, programme, or person — establish this **Model Digital Constitution** as the supreme governing instrument of the enterprise.

We establish it to make our work **safe, traceable, certifiable, sustainable, and accountable** across its whole life, from first concept to final nature-sustainment; to keep authority answerable to evidence rather than to assertion; and to ensure that every artefact we produce can be traced to a requirement, an owner, a configuration, and a record.

---

## 1. Nature of this instrument

This Constitution is the **root governance reference** of Q-plus. It occupies authority rank `0`: no other document, model, rule, or artefact in the repository overrides it. The `01_OPTIONS_ARCHITECTURE` and everything within it — Organizations, Programmes, Technologies, Infrastructures, Operations, Neural-Networks, Standards — derive their authority from this Constitution and are subordinate to it.

Where any lower instrument conflicts with this Constitution, this Constitution prevails, and the lower instrument is the defect to be corrected.

## 2. Foundational values

These values are binding on all Q-plus work and may not be set aside by convenience, schedule, or cost.

| Value | Constitutional meaning |
|---|---|
| **Safety First** | Human safety, system safety, airworthiness, and regulatory compliance are supreme and non-negotiable. No other value may override safety. |
| **Traceability** | Every decision maps to a requirement, evidence, authority, configuration, lifecycle state, and accountable owner. The untraceable is the ungoverned. |
| **Certification Readiness** | Outputs are structured for verification, validation, audit, and authority review from the beginning, not retrofitted. |
| **Sustainability by Design** | Environmental performance and end-of-life recovery are designed in from concept, through to nature-sustainment. |
| **Technical Sovereignty** | Critical data, models, configurations, evidence, and capability remain under controlled governance. |
| **Democratic Enterprise Governance** | Stakeholders participate through structured rights, responsibilities, and controlled decision mechanisms. |
| **Realistic Ambition** | Advanced objectives are decomposed into gated, auditable, achievable work. |

## 3. Scope

This Constitution governs the full lifecycle of Q-plus products and the artefacts that define, build, publish, and sustain them — concept, requirements, architecture, design, verification, certification, production, operation, maintenance, retirement, and circularity. It governs the structures (PBS and its companions), the publication layers (SSOT and PUB), the technology register (Q+ATLANTIDE), the lifecycle model (LC-letter), and the evidence framework (IEF).

## 4. Doctrine of two powers

This Constitution distinguishes two powers, and they shall not be conflated.

- **Constituent power** — the authority to establish and amend this Constitution itself. It is exercised only through the defined amendment procedure (`00-04`) and ratification (`00-05`), never by ordinary engineering or programme decision.
- **Constituted power** — the authority established *by* this Constitution and exercised within it: the Q-Divisions, ORB functions, programmes, and governance bodies. Constituted power may act only within the bounds this Constitution sets; it cannot amend the instrument that grants it.

No exercise of constituted power may rewrite the constituent rules. A programme cannot, by its own decision, set aside a value or rule of this Constitution; it may only propose an amendment through the constituent procedure.

## 5. Supremacy and the No-AAA rule

This Constitution carries the **No-AAA rule**: `AAA` is not a valid domain, division, architecture, interface, enterprise function, or taxonomy element anywhere in Q-plus. This rule is constitutional and may not be overridden by any lower instrument.

The supremacy order is:

```text
00_MODEL-DIGITAL-CONSTITUTION   (authority rank 0 — this instrument)
└── 01_OPTIONS_ARCHITECTURE     (derives authority; subordinate)
    └── all programmes, products, artefacts, rules
```

## 6. Effect

This Preamble has constitutional force as the opening declaration of the Model Digital Constitution. The operative provisions follow in:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| `00-02_ARTICLES` | The articles — binding operative provisions. |
| `00-03_GOVERNANCE-BODIES` | The bodies that exercise constituted power. |
| `00-04_AMENDMENT-PROCEDURE` | How constituent power amends this Constitution. |
| `00-05_RATIFICATION-AND-EVIDENCE` | Ratification and the evidence anchoring this instrument. |

This Preamble takes effect upon ratification (`00-05`) and may be amended only through the constituent procedure (`00-04`).
